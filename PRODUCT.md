## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite (existing app). The landing page lives in the same app at `/`; the reader moves to its own route (`?src=` links keep working). Desktop browsers first (Safari, Zen/Firefox, Chrome).

## What it is

petu is a tutor that explains a PDF by performing on it. You open a PDF, ask a question, and it answers in narrated captions while it scrolls to the right page, highlights the lines it is talking about, and draws to explain, on the page or on a board beside it. Every mark lands on the word that refers to it, and the diagram builds as the explanation goes.

## Positioning (founder, 2026-10-30)

petu is heading toward a personal AI tutor. It is not an "ask/chat with your PDF" tool and must never be positioned or compared as one. Today it starts from the student's own material (a PDF), but the brand is the tutor, not the document: no public copy like "ask your PDF", "explain your PDF" or "ask your slides".

## Users and job

Students working through dense PDFs (lecture slides, textbooks, papers) who want to understand *why*, not get a summary. North star: serious intuition learning, where the student sees the mechanism.

## Differentiator

The answer is performed on the student's own document: speech, pointing and drawing happen together, keyed word by word. A chatbot replies in a text box next to the PDF; petu works inside it.

## Current entry flow

No auth yet. Open a PDF (button, drag and drop, or `?src=` URL), or try the sample deck, then ask questions. Auth and signup come later.
