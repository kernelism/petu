# Landing (the no-document start screen at /)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: students working through dense material. Action: Start with your notes (opens a PDF), or drop one anywhere. The founder locked the mockup .impeccable/mocks/full/ink2/index.html on 2026-10-30 after three rounds; the build must match it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal tutor that shows you why, demonstrated by one real-looking lesson performed live. It refuses the AI-tutor rut (purple gradients, orbs, feature-card grids) and "ask your PDF" framing.
OWN-WORLD: Off-white paper, black line art and hairline page rules with small + marks at the crossings. The single colour is petu's ultramarine ink (#2B44FF), with nib-like strokes that taper and dry lighter. Display type is Bricolage Grotesque (condensed, high optical size), maths is STIX Two, and the student's slide is Helvetica. The board is clean and engine-like, as in the real app.
STORY: The visitor watches petu answer "Why is it divided by 2?" (the 1+…+n staircase), sees it generalise to every n, and sees the lesson slowed down (ask, page, mark, board, check). Then they bring their own lecture.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Headline "The tutor that shows you why." on the left with an ink swash under "why", a short lede, then the "Start with your notes" and "Watch it again" buttons. On the right is the app-shaped stage: the slide with the caption dock under it and the paper board beside it, where the lesson plays.
FORM: Ink on the Page v2, fourth of the round-1 grounded list (founder-chosen over the roll). seed:fcdaf950460d9b89
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
